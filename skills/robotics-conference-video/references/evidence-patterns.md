# General academic evidence patterns

Choose a view because it makes a claim inspectable. No pattern below prescribes a research field, physical structure, technical method, or application.

## Source categories

Distinguish directly observed records, computational/simulated output, derived figures, and explanatory graphics. Record origin, authorization, conditions, selection, and transformations. Do not infer evidence type from visual appearance.

## Comparisons

Document condition labels, comparable inputs/evaluation criteria, shared clock or alignment event, matching view and display scale, playback factors, and justified exceptions. Do not selectively change timing or visual emphasis to favor a condition.

A selected example illustrates that example; aggregation requires the underlying dataset and stated sample size. Show material boundaries rather than implying universal performance.

## Time-based media

Define source in/out points and preserve relevant context. Record whether sources share a measurement clock or only an editorial display clock. Multi-view presentation does not itself establish simultaneous acquisition or causality.

An explanatory detail inset should identify its source and relationship to the main view. A crop must not remove information that changes the inference.

## Data and figures

Keep units, axes, scale, uncertainty, aggregation, and definitions intact. When animating a figure, preserve ordering and meaning. Use consistent scales for comparisons or clearly explain differences. Do not turn annotations into measurements.

## Simulations and explanatory graphics

Identify simulated and explanatory material explicitly. Preserve the conditions and display conventions needed for interpretation. Never use generated graphics to fabricate an observed result.

## Evidence map fields

Record claim ID, exact supported statement, source ID, paper locator, asset ID, usable range, conditions, caveat, and editing decision. Retain source hashes where appropriate. A claim with insufficient support is omitted or qualified.
